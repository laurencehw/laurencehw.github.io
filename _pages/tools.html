Tools

Interactive research tools I have built. Code is on GitHub. The open textbooks, including The African Economy and its data tools, are listed on their own page.

Interactive

SA Policy Space
research tool on South Africa's economic-reform agenda · updated weekly
171 reform ideas, each traced to the parliamentary committee record that raised it and to the published research that bears on it, kept current by weekly harvests of Parliament, calls for comment, and the research literature. With a reform index, a weekly briefing, and a question tool whose answers are grounded in that evidence base.
Bridging African Borders
cross-border investment policy in Africa, traced to the documents · launched October 2026, public draft
The policy blockers to cross-border investment in Africa, the reforms that would remove them, and how far each has got. Every idea is tracked on four tracks, legal, rules, delivery and funding, with a dated status for each and a citation that says whether a person has checked it. Deep coverage so far of cross-border payments and currency, regional power trade in Southern Africa, and transport corridors and border posts, organized by policy area, region, country and stakeholder. The African sibling of SA Policy Space.
EconWar
simulation platform for economic statecraft
Simulates events involving global economic coercive measures — sanctions, export controls, tariffs, and investment restrictions — and how targets and third countries respond.
Balance of Power
the global monetary system, measured · launched October 2026, in development
Charts of the dollar system, each rebuilt from a public source and, where marked, checked against a figure the source itself published. Organized in three layers: the hierarchy of money: what the dollar costs, who can borrow it when it runs short, and how many months of imports the periphery can cover; the dollar's transaction cycle: the currency of pricing, financing, risk transfer, payment and reinvestment; and the economic statecraft toolbox: the instruments the United States has used. It measures the past and makes no forecasts. The data companion to Global Finance: A Money View.
Fiscal Policy Calculator
budget and macroeconomic scoring of US tax and spending proposals · source
Estimates the budgetary and macroeconomic effects of US tax and spending proposals from IRS and FRED data, following CBO conventions, with static, behavioral, and dynamic scoring.
