Tools

Interactive tools I have built, and open textbook drafts. Code is on GitHub.

Interactive

SA Policy Space
research tool on South Africa's economic-reform agenda · updated weekly
171 reform ideas, each traced to the parliamentary committee record that raised it and to the published research that bears on it, kept current by weekly harvests of Parliament, calls for comment, and the research literature. With a reform index, a weekly briefing, and a question tool whose answers are grounded in that evidence base.
The African Economy: The Growth Century
website-textbook with interactive data · first edition, September 2026
Treats Africa as the growth economy of the rest of this century, organized around the systems that cross borders: corridors, currency zones, city-regions, value chains, payment rails. With nine data tools and 54 country cards: a century slider for population 1950–2100, a growth-pole map, a sovereign debt tracker, an AfCFTA explorer, and a convergence calculator.
EconWar
simulation platform for economic statecraft
Simulates events involving global economic coercive measures — sanctions, export controls, tariffs, and investment restrictions — and how targets and third countries respond.
Fiscal Policy Calculator
budget and macroeconomic scoring of US tax and spending proposals · source
Estimates the budgetary and macroeconomic effects of US tax and spending proposals from IRS and FRED data, following CBO conventions, with static, behavioral, and dynamic scoring.

Open textbooks

Living drafts, published as they are written at laurence-wilse-samson.gitbook.io. The ones furthest along:

Economic Coercion
The tools of economic statecraft; companion to EconWar.
Institutionalist Macro: An American Approach
A measurement-first introduction built around the national accounts and the flow of funds.
Institutional Microeconomics
Markets, firms, and non-market arrangements as institutions for allocating resources, from the price mechanism and why firms exist through information, market design, and the commons.
Public Economics
Public finance theory, causal evidence and the actual rules of US programs, with the normative questions (Rawls, Sen, Nozick) kept in view; the text for the Wagner course.
Global Finance: A Money View
International finance as balance sheets, dealers and a hierarchy of money, after Perry Mehrling, where liquidity rather than solvency is the binding constraint.
Financial Economics: Claims, Prices, Holders
Treats the claims firms issue, the prices they trade at and the investors who hold them as one subject.
Empirical Research Methods
Organized around research questions rather than techniques, with quantitative and qualitative methods as complements, and R and Python code throughout.
Antitrust Research Methods
A working manual that sets econometrics beside case law, qualitative evidence and agency practice across the US, EU and South Africa, with every figure reproducible in R; the text for the CCRED course.
World Economic History
From prehistory to the present, pairing each era with how we know what we know: the papers, data and identification strategies behind the evidence.
The South African Economy
The democratic era from 1994 to 2025 in three parts: macroeconomics and state capacity, the productive sectors, and labour, skills and distribution.
The American Economy and The Chinese Economy
Sector-by-sector institutional portraits; The African Economy above is the third in the series.
