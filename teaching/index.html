Teaching

I teach four courses, all at the master's level.

  • Public Economics — NYU Wagner Graduate School of Public Service.
  • Labor Policy: The Future of Work — NYU Wagner. How institutions should respond to AI-driven disruption of labor markets.
  • Understanding Emerging Technologies — Columbia School of International and Public Affairs. AI governance, export controls, data privacy, platform economics, and the politics of emerging-technology regulation.
  • Research Methods in Competition Policy and Economic Regulation — Centre for Competition, Regulation and Economic Development, University of Johannesburg. A practitioner course for competition authorities and consultants; I also supervise master's dissertations there.

I have also taught the Economics of International Development and Urban Economics at NYU Wagner, and at the University of Cape Town. Several courses draw on the open textbooks listed under Tools.
