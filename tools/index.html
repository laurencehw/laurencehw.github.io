Laurence Wilse-Samson Research Teaching Tools CV

Tools

Interactive tools I have built, and open textbook drafts. Code is on GitHub.

Interactive

  • EconWar — a platform for simulating events involving global economic coercive measures: sanctions, export controls, tariffs, and investment restrictions, and how targets and third countries respond.
  • Fiscal Policy Calculator — estimates the budgetary and macroeconomic effects of US tax and spending proposals from IRS and FRED data, following CBO conventions, with static, behavioral, and dynamic scoring. Source.
  • SA Policy Space — a database of South Africa's economic-reform agenda: 170+ proposals traced to the parliamentary record and the research that bears on them, with a weighted reform index and an evidence-grounded question tool.

Open textbooks

Living drafts, published as they are written at laurence-wilse-samson.gitbook.io. The ones furthest along:

  • Economic Coercion — the tools of economic statecraft; companion to EconWar.
  • Institutionalist Macro: An American Approach and Institutional Microeconomics — measurement-first introductions built around the national accounts and the flow of funds.
  • Public Economics — the text for the Wagner course.
  • Global Finance: A Money View and Financial Economics: Claims, Prices, Holders.
  • Empirical Research Methods and Antitrust Research Methods — the latter is the text for the CCRED course.
