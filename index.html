Laurence Wilse-Samson

I am an economist in New York. I am Associate Director at Oxford Economics, where I lead applied research for technology and financial-services clients, and I teach as an adjunct at NYU Wagner and Columbia SIPA. I am also a Senior Research Fellow at the University of Johannesburg's Centre for Competition, Regulation and Economic Development.

My research is on how labor markets absorb technological change, on the measurement and effectiveness of industrial policy, and on economic history, with a long-standing interest in South Africa, where I'm from. Before Oxford Economics I was Director of Policy Research at Bird, an Associate at Cornerstone Research, and a research assistant to Joseph Stiglitz at Columbia, where I took my PhD in 2014.
